# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Apender project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming different opinions and perspectives.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Helping newcomers feel welcome.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting or threatening other contributors.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or disruptive behavior.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and pull requests.
- Discussions and comments.
- Project-related communication channels.
- Any other space where someone represents the Apender project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it through the GitHub repository:

https://github.com/Nic24nix/apender/issues

Please include as much relevant information as possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will review reports and take appropriate action.

They may remove inappropriate content, reject contributions, or restrict participation in the project when necessary.
